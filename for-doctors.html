09 / Before you begin

Questions before
you get started?

Clear answers about your content, your practice and your plan.

13 questions

Getting startedWhat will DigiDr do for my practice?+

DigiDr brings your professional profile, microsite, patient facing content and digital practice tools together. Your plan determines content volumes, languages, appointment tools, referral features and analytics.

Getting startedHow do I get started?+

Register your interest with your name, specialty and contact details. The DigiDr team takes the request forward and prepares onboarding. Have your credentials, clinic information, consultation preferences and approved professional account details ready.

Plans & billingIs Lite free, and can I choose a paid plan directly?+

The published Lite offer is free, with no card required. You can start with Lite or discuss Classic, Premium or Pro during onboarding. Lite supports appointment requests; online booking and payments are listed on the paid plans.

Content & visibilityDoes content go live without my approval?+

No. The current plan comparison includes doctor approval before publication on every plan. You review the proposed content before it is released to the agreed professional channels.

Content & visibilityCan I use a regional language?+

Classic and Lite list English. Premium adds one regional language; Pro adds Hindi and one regional language. Confirm the language and channel combination you need during onboarding.

Website & practiceCan patients book different consultation types?+

The microsite offering describes in clinic, video and home consultation options. Enable only the modes you actually provide. Online booking and payments are paid plan features; Lite provides appointment requests.

Content & visibilityWill DigiDr guarantee search rankings or patient numbers?+

No ranking or patient volume promise is made here. Structured professional information and consistent, useful content support discoverability. Search indexing, rankings and patient decisions depend on factors outside DigiDr’s control.

Website & practiceWho owns my profile and content?+

Your professional identity and content remain associated with you. DigiDr hosts and operates the service. Review the service terms and confirm hosting, export and deletion arrangements before committing to a plan.

Plans & billingWhen does a paid plan start, and how do upgrades work?+

The published pricing FAQ describes activation on the first of the coming month and an upgrade from the following month. Your quote should state the start date, any upgrade adjustment and the complete payable amount before you pay.

Plans & billingDo the prices include GST, and are there setup fees?+

The current pricing page describes GST inclusive prices and no setup fee. Ask for an itemised quote and invoice confirming the charges and terms that apply to your subscription.

Plans & billingWhat is the difference between six and twelve months?+

The plan features are the same; the term and commercial offer differ. Six month introductory amounts are shown above. Request a confirmed annual total and subsequent pricing schedule before choosing twelve months.

Plans & billingWhat happens at renewal or if I want to cancel?+

The current pricing FAQ describes manual renewal. Check your billing notice for the amount and dates. Before payment, confirm cancellation, refund and continued hosting terms with DigiDr; the policy links below are available for reference.

Getting startedWhere can I get help with my account?+

Use the existing web app to access your account, or contact DigiDr at info@digidr.app for onboarding and general support. For billing or data requests, include the relevant account or invoice reference and avoid sending unnecessary patient information.
