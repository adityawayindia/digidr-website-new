Product perspective · Connected Journey

From a Post to a Patient

A useful health explanation should be able to become the beginning of a doctor relationship rather than an isolated piece of marketing content.

Trusted Discovery is valuable because it can connect information to accountable professional care.

The content should identify the doctor behind it

A patient needs to understand who is associated with the explanation and what that practice actually does.

The next step should preserve context

If the patient asks a question, the interaction should not forget what brought them there.

Engagement should not equal conversion pressure

The correct next step may be a different service, clinic contact or no appointment at all.

The doctor relationship is the durable unit

Content, conversation and preparation become more useful when they remain connected to the same professional context.
