Technology

Healthcare AI needs more than an answer.

The hard problem is maintaining meaning and authority around the answer: context, evidence, permission, model behavior, tools, human review and external actions.

Context · evidence · authority

Three typed boundaries

Illustrative system flow

  1. 01

    Permitted task

    Confirm who is asking, why, and what this task may use.

  2. 02

    Relevant context

    ContextPack carries the permitted records that matter now.

  3. 03

    Assessed evidence

    EvidencePackage makes support, disagreement and limits inspectable.

  4. 04

    Action decision

    TrustDecision allows, restricts, requests review or stops the operation.

Permission is checked before context is assembled and again before an external effect.

ContextPack

Purpose specific context assembled only after the current task is permitted to use it.

EvidencePackage

Assessed support, contradiction, applicability and uncertainty for the medical meaning being communicated.

TrustDecision

Allow, restrict, require review, block or escalate for a particular operation and artifact.

Design principles

Authorization before relevance: and again before effectInformation can be useful and still be unavailable for the current purpose.
Deterministic where the rule is knownIdentity, permissions, exact versions, budgets and state transitions belong in ordinary software.
Models are qualified by operationA model suitable for shortening approved content does not automatically qualify for conflicting evidence synthesis.
Economic bounds are part of correctnessAgentic work that creates unbounded calls or review burden is not operationally correct.
