Trust Architecture

Trust is not a score. It is a system of evidence, context and authority.

DigiDr separates traceability, relevance, freshness, safety, transparency, evidence grounding and doctor authority so one reassuring badge never hides what actually happened.

TRUSTED Healthcare

Traceable

Can we reconstruct the information, transformations and authority behind this result?

Relevant

Does the permitted information fit this person, practice, purpose and moment?

Up to date

Is the information current enough for the intended use?

Safe

Is the proposed response or action inside an evaluated operating boundary?

Transparent

Can the user understand source, AI involvement, review and limitations?

Evidence grounded

Do assessed sources support the medical meaning being communicated?

Doctor governed

Does the doctor retain meaningful authority over consequential communication?

From principle to practice

Trust follows the exact version.

A source, a review and a release stay connected. When the medical meaning changes, the review must change with it.

Illustrative system flow

  1. 01

    Trace the basis

    Keep the source and the medical claim connected.

  2. 02

    Inspect the evidence

    Make support, applicability and uncertainty visible.

  3. 03

    Review the version

    The doctor inspects the exact communication and its scope.

  4. 04

    Permit the release

    Release or reuse only within the approved meaning and purpose.

Illustrative state: the reviewed version may proceed within its approved scope.

Changed meaning, evidence or scope returns the communication to review. A previous approval is not a reusable badge.
