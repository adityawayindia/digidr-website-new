BEFORE THE VISIT

Answer the practical
questions first.

Patients can find preparation details and understand what happens next without searching through unrelated information.

What information appears in this section?+

The real practice can provide its address, consultation hours, supported visit modes, fees and appointment instructions.

Can a patient request or book a visit?+

Lite offers appointment requests. Paid plans list online booking and payments. The microsite should make the distinction clear and show confirmation only after the relevant action succeeds.

Can this preview book an appointment?+

No. This is an interactive website example. It does not collect patient information, show real availability, take payment or send a booking request.
