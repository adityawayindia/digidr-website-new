A citation is not the same as support.

EvidenceEngine maintains source versions, medically meaningful claims, assessments, qualifications, contradictions and the downstream artifacts that depend on them.

How EvidenceEngine works

A claim needs a lifecycle.

Connect a medical claim to its source versions, assessment and downstream uses. Make support, disagreement and change inspectable.

Select a stage to inspect its role.

Assess the assertion that actually matters.

Separate the medically meaningful claim from surrounding wording, style and format.

Keep the qualifications that change its meaning attached to the claim.

EvidenceEngine · 04 / 05

Provide assessed support for the current task.

The EvidencePackage brings together the claim, relevant source versions, assessments and material qualifications.

The same medical meaning can appear in an article, caption, translation and patient explanation. A derivative that removes a material qualification should not inherit approval by textual similarity alone.

EvidenceEngine · 05 / 05

Find the content affected by new evidence.

Track which downstream artifacts depend on a changed claim or source.

Reassess the affected meaning and route consequential content changes back through review.

A changed source or qualification returns the affected claim to assessment and review.

Change should trigger proportionate repair.

Source corrected or withdrawnRestrict eligible reuse and identify dependent claims.
New evidence adds uncertaintyReassess the claim without pretending the older explanation was automatically false.
Downstream variants affectedLocate article, social, translation and reusable answer dependencies.
Insufficient supportNarrow, qualify, research further or stop instead of searching downward for a weaker citation.
