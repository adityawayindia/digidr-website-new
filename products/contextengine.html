Remember what matters: and know when not to use it.

ContextEngine maintains doctor, patient, clinic and specialty context as attributable records and assembles purpose specific ContextPacks only after current permission is established.

ContextEngine · 04 / 04

Give the task enough context and visible limits.

The pack should contain enough information to perform the task and enough metadata to understand what is missing: without treating a compressed summary as a complete medical record.

ContextPack is a task contract: what may be used, why it belongs and what remains unresolved.
