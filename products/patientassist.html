Product 02 · PatientAssist

A conversation that carries
understanding forward.

A virtual assistant that asks progressively useful questions, offers guidance approved by the doctor and keeps the patient’s concerns connected before and after the visit.

  1. 1

    Understand the real intent

    Begin with what the patient is trying to accomplish, before asking for more detail.

  2. 2

    Ask the question that matters

    Choose the next question when its answer improves understanding or the next step.

  3. 3

    Curate trusted guidance

    Bring relevant articles and preparation guidance approved by the doctor into the conversation.

  4. 4

    Continue after the visit

    Keep progress updates and follow up questions connected, so the patient can build on what is known.

relationship continuous.

How PatientAssist works

From a question to a clearer next step.

Understand the purpose, ask only what helps and prepare a summary the patient can check. The conversation ends with a useful next route.

Select a stage to inspect its role.

PatientAssist · 01 / 05

Begin with what the person needs.

The patient should understand when they are interacting with AI and when a doctor needs to participate.

Clarify whether the purpose is practice fit, preparation, a question for the clinic or an appointment route.

PatientAssist · 02 / 05

Bring forward only permitted, useful details.

Use the minimum context the current task is permitted to access.

Unknown, not asked, declined, patient reported and doctor confirmed are different states.

Illustrative output

Context with its source

Clearly attributed
Unknowns

Preparation does not turn a report into a confirmed finding.

PatientAssist · 03 / 05

Ask what changes understanding. Stop when it doesn’t.

Question selection should be driven by the current purpose, missing information and patient burden.

Ask a useful follow up when the answer could change the preparation or next route. Avoid collecting detail simply because it is available.

PatientAssist · 04 / 05

Make the summary easy to correct.

Reason for contact, chronology, main concern, recent changes and open questions should remain correctable.

Keep the patient’s wording and distinguish their reports from anything confirmed by a doctor.

PatientAssist · 05 / 05

End with a useful next step.

The appropriate route may be booking, clinic contact, doctor review or a different accountable path.

When professional judgment is needed, prepare the context for the doctor; do not present an AI response as the doctor’s decision.

The patient can correct the summary before it is used for the next step.

AI can prepare the work. It should not impersonate the doctor.

When a question requires professional judgment, the system can organize relevant context and prepare grounded draft communication. The doctor can inspect, edit, replace, defer or ask for more information before release.
