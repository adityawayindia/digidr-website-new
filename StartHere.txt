DigiDr Website: Version 16.0.0

START HERE

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. Do not open index.html from inside
   the ZIP preview, and do not separate it from the assets folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox for a quick local review.
3. To publish, upload the contents of this folder to your static website host.
   Keep all subfolders and relative paths intact.

This package contains the website and local visual assets. External app,
registration, email and reference links require an internet connection.
The static website does not create real appointments or process payments.

The main homepage includes the three Doctor stories after DoctorAssist and
before Research. Open index.html#doctor-stories to jump to the carousel.
The supplied outline avatars and carousel controls are included.

For editable source, build tools and review reports, use the separate
DigiDrWebsiteV16Source.zip package. Its hosting files are in dist/.

RELEASE-MANIFEST.json records the byte count and SHA256 of every included file.
